From *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering*: the people who do great work are those who keep asking what the important problems in their field are, and who keep a few of them in mind even while working on something else. The advice is easy to agree with and hard to follow, because it trades the comfort of the tractable for the possibility of the significant.
