title: A one-line proof that √2 is irrational
date: 2026-11-05
tags: [number theory, proofs]
---

Suppose $\sqrt2 = p/q$ with $q$ as small as possible. Then $\sqrt2 = (2q - p)/(p - q)$, and since $q < p < 2q$, the new denominator is positive and smaller than $q$. Contradiction. No parity argument needed, and the same trick works for $\sqrt n$ whenever $n$ is not a perfect square.
