title: Kelly is just the geometric mean
date: 2026-10-12
tags: [probability, betting]
---

The Kelly criterion maximises $\mathbb{E}[\log W]$, which is the same as maximising the long-run geometric growth rate of wealth. For a bet paying $b$ to 1 with win probability $p$, the optimal stake is

$$
f^* = p - \frac{1 - p}{b}.
$$

Everything else about Kelly, including its famous aggressiveness, follows from the fact that the logarithm punishes ruin infinitely.
